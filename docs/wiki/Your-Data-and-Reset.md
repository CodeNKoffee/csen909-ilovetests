# Your data and reset

- There is no server and no account. Demo progress is saved in your browser's local storage under `ilovetests-demo-v1`.
- One browser holds one demo, so a single tester can play every role.
- **Reset demo** in the guide, then **Yes, reset**, starts again from step 1.
- A private window, clearing site data or another browser also starts fresh.
- All pupils, marks and AI output are fictional, fixed data in `lib/data.ts`.
