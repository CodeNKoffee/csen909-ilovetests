  1 Open “Hand in my work”
    1.1 See the due card from Lukki on Home
  2 Solve on paper
    2.1 Write the working steps
    2.2 Write the final answer
  3 Take photos in the wizard
    3.1 Snap each page inside the frame
    3.2 [System] App checks blur and readability
    3.3 Retake the page, then back to 3.2: the app checks it again
  4 Send to Mrs Rasha
    4.1 Tap “Send to Mrs Rasha”
  Steps removed from the HTA: Find the post in Classroom (1.1–1.3); Check each photo by eye (3.2); Open Classroom, attach, turn in (4.1–4.3); Hand in the paper copy (5)

HTD 2 · Teacher or assistant reads and grades a batch (Mrs Rasha · Hatem (assistant))
Plan 0: Do 1–5 in order. Hatem (assistant) can only save drafts and send them on; only Mrs Rasha confirms grades and decides “not in the mark scheme”.
States: AI draft -> Assistant draft (not a grade) -> Teacher-confirmed grade -> Released result
  1 Open the batch
    1.1 Tap the next action on Home
  2 Check the text the AI read
    2.0 [System] AI reads every answer; least certain first
    2.1 Confirm or correct each reading
    2.2 Mark unreadable answers (0 marks)
  3 Review the AI marks
    3.0 [System] AI marks each step and flags “Second look”
    3.1 Confirm, or tap steps to override [Teacher]
    3.2 Save the marks as a draft (not a grade) [Assistant]
    3.3 “Not in the mark scheme”: assistant sends it to the teacher; only she decides the marks [Teacher decides]
  4 Leave comments
    4.1 Edit the suggested comment
    4.2 Add a general comment
  5 Confirm the grades
    5.1 Tap “Confirm all grades”: on only once every answer is confirmed [Teacher]
    5.2 Tap “Send draft to Mrs Rasha”: drafts only [Assistant]
  Steps removed from the HTA: Hand off by phone and WhatsApp (1.1–1.3); Solve each question ourselves (3.4); Make out handwriting alone (2.2); Return the batch and tell the teacher (5.1–5.2)
