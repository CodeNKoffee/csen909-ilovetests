HTD 2 · Teacher or assistant reads and grades a batch (Mrs Rasha · Hatem (assistant))
Phase 2 HTA: 5 tasks, 17 steps
0. Teacher or assistant reads and grades a batch
Plan 0: Do 1–5 in order. Hatem stops at 5 with “Send draft”; only Mrs Rasha confirms.
  1. Open the batch
     1.1 Tap the next action on Home
  2. Check the text the AI read
     2.0 [System] AI reads every answer; least certain first
     2.1 Confirm or correct each reading
     2.2 Mark unreadable answers (0 marks)
  3. Review the AI marks
     3.0 [System] AI marks each step and flags “Second look”
     3.1 Confirm, or tap steps to override
     3.2 “Not in the mark scheme” → own judgement
  4. Leave comments
     4.1 Edit the suggested comment
     4.2 Add a general comment
  5. Confirm all grades
     5.1 Tap “Confirm all” (assistant: “Send draft”)
Gone from the HTA: Hand off by phone and WhatsApp (1.1–1.3); Solve each question ourselves (3.4); Make out handwriting alone (2.2); Return the batch and tell the teacher (5.1–5.2)
